Our Story

From humble beginnings to industry leadership

Building a Legacy of Quality

Founded with a vision to revolutionize the custom printing industry, Karunex Co. Pvt. Ltd. began as a small workshop with big dreams. Our founders, industry veterans with decades of experience, recognized the need for a printing partner that combined traditional craftsmanship with cutting-edge technology.

What started as a local printing service has grown into a comprehensive solutions provider serving businesses across the globe. Our journey has been marked by continuous innovation, unwavering commitment to quality, and an unrelenting focus on customer satisfaction.

2015

Serving 5,000+ satisfied clients worldwide
